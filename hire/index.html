FAQ

What do I send you?

If I'm editing your footage: your raw recordings by Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer, plus your script or voiceover if you have one.

If I'm making the Short for you: just the mod or topic and any points it has to hit. I handle the gameplay, script and edit.

Either way: one video whose style you love (or skip it if you're happy with my own style), any brand assets (logo, fonts, intro or outro), your deadline and where it will be posted.

How do I pay?

Revolut is easiest: a secure link for card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, no account needed. I also take PayPal, Wise and bank transfer. Other terms are open to discussion, but a 50% deposit upfront is non-negotiable. After a first project together, studios and brands can pay the rest by invoice (Net-30).

You're in Australia. Is that a problem?

Not at all, it's a perk. I'm on Brisbane time (UTC+10), so footage you send at the end of your day gets edited while you sleep. I reply within 48 hours and share previews over email or Discord. Revision notes can just be timestamps, like "0:42, cut this line".

Who owns the final video?

You do, once the final payment clears. If I'm editing your footage, it's yours to use anywhere, ads included. If I'm making the Short for you, it's yours to post on your own channels; running it as a paid ad is a separate usage fee, quoted per project. You get the final MP4; source and project files are +$50 a Short. I keep finished work private until you post it, then I can show it in my portfolio. Credit me in the description (like "Edited by @GalaxiHD") unless you've bought paid ad rights.

Can I sponsor a video on your channel?

Yes, as a Short or a long-form integration on GalaxiHD. Email me your brand, product, preferred format and timeline, and I'll reply within 48 hours with rates. Every sponsorship is clearly disclosed, as YouTube and FTC rules require. Using the video in your own ads is quoted separately. My audience numbers are just below.
