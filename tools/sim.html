Boss fight sim

Local only (not linked from the site). Steps every turn of the fight frame by frame with three players: a bot that predicts every bullet a few frames ahead and steps out of the way (a decent human), still (never moves) and random (mashes directions). Fewer bot hits = more readable; the gap to still/random is how much dodging matters. Run: open localhost:8765/tools/sim.html, or call sim.run() / sim.run([2, 4]) from the console. sim.dialogue() plays every route (each menu pick for 2 loops, FIGHT hit/miss, win, flee, 12 deaths + replay, the phone game's three endings) and lists any line in boss.js that never reached the screen. Results are also on window.simResult.
